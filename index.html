What it does

Built for the part of the search nobody enjoys.

Applying is the easy half. Remembering where you applied, who went quiet, and which version of your resume you sent is the half that falls apart in a spreadsheet.

Log a job in seconds

Company, role, salary, source, the link, the exact resume you sent. Paste a posting and autofill reads the whole thing for you, so the form is already filled when it opens.

Statuses that mean something

Applied, interviewing, rejected, offered, withdrawn, ghosted. Anything that has gone quiet too long is marked for you, so silence stops looking like hope.

Interviews and nudges

Schedule an interview with its meeting link and location, and get reminded two hours out. Follow-up prompts appear when an application has been waiting too long.

Analytics from your own entries

Response rate, where your interviews actually come from, how long each stage takes, and which sources waste your time. Computed on your device from what you logged.

Your resumes, kept straight

Store the versions you send, see exactly what a parser reads out of each PDF, and attach the right one to the right application. Reading the parse costs nothing.

Export whenever you want

Everything comes out as an Excel workbook or plain CSV, created on your device. Nothing is locked in, and there is no copy of it anywhere else to ask for.

Privacy

There is no server holding your job hunt.

Offerly has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you type lives in a database on your device, and in your own iCloud if you switch sync on. We cannot read any of it, because there is nowhere for us to read it from.

The exception is the AI tools. When you tap one, the text it needs goes to Google to be processed and the answer comes back. Nothing about you is sent in the background.

The whole list of who it talks to

  • Apple, for iCloud sync if you turn it on, Maps lookups on addresses you type, and purchases
  • Google, for the AI tools, and only when you tap one

Questions

The things people ask first.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no password. Open the app and start logging.

Does it work without internet?

Everything except the AI tools works offline, including logging, editing, analytics, reminders and export. The AI tools need a connection because the models run on Google's side.

Do I have to turn on iCloud?

No. iCloud sync is a switch in Settings, off unless you turn it on. With it on, your applications follow you to your other devices through your own private iCloud database.

Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

The PDF itself is never uploaded. When you run a tool that needs it, the text read out of that one resume is sent to be processed, and nothing else. The file stays on your device.

What happens when I run out of AI runs?

The tool tells you how many you have left and when the count resets, and the rest of the app carries on as normal. Allowances reset at the start of each month, and a run that fails without being processed is given back.

How do I cancel Momentum?

In the Settings app on your device, under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the renewal and the AI tools keep working until the period you paid for ends.
